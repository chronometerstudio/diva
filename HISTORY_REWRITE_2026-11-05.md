# History rewrite of 5 November 2026

On 5 November 2026 the commit history of this repository was rewritten for one reason: earlier commits carried a personal e-mail address in their author and committer fields. All commits now carry the address of the account `chronometerstudio`.

Nothing else changed. File contents, file trees, commit dates and commit messages are identical; references to old commit identifiers inside commit messages were left as they were, and the table below resolves them. Every SHA-256 commitment in this repository (`SOURCE_SHA256.txt`, `PRIORITY_SHA256.txt`, `PREREG_SHA256.txt`) is therefore unaffected.

The dates of the main commitments are also fixed independently of this history, by the Zenodo records 10.5281/zenodo.23499372 (archive of this repository, 20 October 2026), 10.5281/zenodo.23539231 (priority note, 21 October 2026) and 10.5281/zenodo.23550288 (research note v0.2, 22 October 2026).

The two root commits of 20 October 2026 differed only in their author field; they are now one commit. The tags `v2026-10-20` and `v2026-10-20.1` point to it.
